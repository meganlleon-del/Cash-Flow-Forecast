Formulas used

Cash inflows = Monthly sales revenue × Cash collection %
Cash outflows = Operating expenses + Inventory purchases + Loan payments
Net monthly cash flow = Cash inflows − Cash outflows
Ending cash balance = Beginning cash balance + Net monthly cash flow
Surplus / (deficit) = Ending cash balance
Additional financing needed = MAX(0, −Ending cash balance)

How to interpret the forecast

Cash collections are forecast at 80% of sales, producing $160,000 in cash inflows. After $150,000 of cash outflows, the month generates $10,000 in net cash flow. Starting with $30,000, the business ends with $40,000 in cash.

This is a simplified one-month planning tool. It assumes all listed expenses and purchases are paid during the month and excludes taxes, interest not included in loan payments, and other cash movements.
